UNIVERSITY OF HAWAIʻI AT MĀNOA
INFORMATION AND COMPUTER SCIENCES (ICS) COMPLETE CATALOG & SCHEDULE DATA

This file compiles all undergraduate and graduate ICS courses, listing typical
weekday time blocks, recurring course assignments, professors, and aggregated 
student community ratings (Scale: 1.0 - 5.0 Quality, where 5.0 is highest).

------------------------------------------------------------------------
100-LEVEL: INTRODUCTORY & FOUNDATIONAL
------------------------------------------------------------------------

ICS 101: Digital Tools for the Information World (4 Credits)
  - Prerequisites: None.
  - Typical Weekday Blocks: TR 9:00 AM - 10:15 AM, TR 10:30 AM - 11:45 AM
  - Faculty Assignment: Department Pool / Lab Lecturers
  - Student Quality Rating: 3.5 / 5.0
  - Community Notes: Heavy emphasis on Microsoft Office suite mastery. High workload but straightforward grading.

ICS 102: Introduction to Data Science (3 Credits)
  - Prerequisites: None.
  - Typical Weekday Blocks: MWF 10:30 AM - 11:20 AM
  - Faculty Assignment: Mahdi Belcaid
  - Student Quality Rating: 4.1 / 5.0
  - Community Notes: Great introductory look at data parsing, visualization, and python environments.

ICS 103: Computer Science Principles (3 Credits)
  - Prerequisites: None.
  - Typical Weekday Blocks: TR 12:00 PM - 1:15 PM
  - Faculty Assignment: Department Lecturers
  - Student Quality Rating: 3.8 / 5.0
  - Community Notes: Concept-focused, minimal programming, ideal for testing out the major field.

ICS 110: Introduction to Computer Science I (3 Credits)
  - Prerequisites: None.
  - Typical Weekday Blocks: MWF 11:30 AM - 12:20 PM
  - Faculty Assignment: Department Staff
  - Student Quality Rating: 4.0 / 5.0
  - Community Notes: For true beginners before taking ICS 111. Highly accessible.

ICS 111: Introduction to Computer Science I (4 Credits)
  - Prerequisites: MATH 134 or MATH 140 or math placement test.
  - Typical Weekday Blocks: TR 1:30 PM - 2:45 PM + Scheduled Lab Sessions (W/F)
  - Faculty Assignment: William Albritton / Laura Garrison
  - Student Quality Rating: 4.2 / 5.0 (Albritton)
  - Community Notes: Standard Java foundation. Albritton is highly praised for clear code demos and thorough explanations.

ICS 141: Discrete Mathematics for Computer Science I (3 Credits)
  - Prerequisites: MATH 215 or MATH 241 or concurrent enrollment.
  - Typical Weekday Blocks: MWF 9:30 AM - 10:20 AM
  - Faculty Assignment: David Chin / Torben Podolske
  - Student Quality Rating: 3.4 / 5.0
  - Community Notes: Heavy focus on mathematical logic, proofs, and sets. Demands significant self-study time.

200-LEVEL: INTERMEDIATE CORE
------------------------------------------------------------------------

ICS 211: Introduction to Computer Science II (4 Credits)
  - Prerequisites: ICS 111 with B or better, and ICS 141 with C or better.
  - Typical Weekday Blocks: TR 10:30 AM - 11:45 AM + Scheduled Lab Sessions (M/W)
  - Faculty Assignment: Vern Takebayashi
  - Student Quality Rating: 3.9 / 5.0
  - Community Notes: Data structures in Java (Linked Lists, Trees, Stacks). Takebayashi gives rigorous, helpful feedback.

ICS 212: Program Structure (3 Credits)
  - Prerequisites: ICS 211 with B or better.
  - Typical Weekday Blocks: MW 1:30 PM - 2:45 PM
  - Faculty Assignment: Ravi Narayan
  - Student Quality Rating: 4.5 / 5.0
  - Community Notes: Teaches low-level memory management in C and C++. Narayan is highly regarded as clear, strict, and incredibly practical.

ICS 215: Introduction to Scripting (3 Credits)
  - Prerequisites: ICS 211 with C or better.
  - Typical Weekday Blocks: TR 9:00 AM - 10:15 AM
  - Faculty Assignment: Department Staff
  - Student Quality Rating: 3.7 / 5.0
  - Community Notes: Practical skills focusing on Bash shell syntax, automation tools, and configuration scripting.

ICS 241: Discrete Mathematics for Computer Science II (3 Credits)
  - Prerequisites: ICS 141 with C or better.
  - Typical Weekday Blocks: MWF 1:30 PM - 2:20 PM
  - Faculty Assignment: Torben Podolske / David Chin
  - Student Quality Rating: 3.2 / 5.0
  - Community Notes: Combinatorics, probability, graph theory. Conceptual hurdle for many sophomore students.

300-LEVEL: UPPER-DIVISION REQUIRED CORE
------------------------------------------------------------------------

ICS 311: Algorithms (4 Credits)
  - Prerequisites: ICS 211 with B or better, and ICS 241 with C or better.
  - Typical Weekday Blocks: TR 12:00 PM - 1:15 PM + Mandatory Friday Problem Sessions
  - Faculty Assignment: Nodari Sitchinava
  - Student Quality Rating: 4.3 / 5.0
  - Community Notes: Fast-paced algorithmic time/space analysis. Sitchinava is tough but exceptionally organized.

ICS 312: Machine-Level and Systems Programming (3 Credits)
  - Prerequisites: ICS 212 with C or better.
  - Typical Weekday Blocks: TR 3:00 PM - 4:15 PM
  - Faculty Assignment: Henri Casanova
  - Student Quality Rating: 4.4 / 5.0
  - Community Notes: Deep dive into Assembly and system architecture. Casanova's lectures are meticulously prepared.

ICS 314: Software Engineering I (3 Credits)
  - Prerequisites: ICS 211 with B or better.
  - Typical Weekday Blocks: MW 3:00 PM - 4:15 PM
  - Faculty Assignment: Philip Johnson
  - Student Quality Rating: 4.6 / 5.0
  - Community Notes: Open source development, web stacks, tech portfolio building. Highly structured project timelines.

ICS 321: Database Systems I (3 Credits)
  - Prerequisites: ICS 211 with B or better, and ICS 141 with C or better.
  - Typical Weekday Blocks: TR 1:30 PM - 2:45 PM
  - Faculty Assignment: Kyungim Baek

ICS 332: Operating Systems (3 Credits)

  - Faculty Assignment: Henri Casanova / Lipyeow Lim
  - Student Quality Rating: 4.1 / 5.0

ICS 351: Computer Networks (3 Credits)

  - Typical Weekday Blocks: TR 10:30 AM - 11:45 AM
  - Faculty Assignment: Edoardo Biagioni
  - Student Quality Rating: 3.6 / 5.0
  - Community Notes: Networking routing layers, TCP/IP models, hands-on lab infrastructure building.

ICS 355: Security Science I (3 Credits)
  - Prerequisites: ICS 211 with C or better.
  - Typical Weekday Blocks: MW 4:30 PM - 5:45 PM
  - Faculty Assignment: Benjamin Kuperman
  - Student Quality Rating: 4.3 / 5.0
  - Community Notes: Introduction to systems security, cryptography concepts, vulnerabilities, and offensive/defensive methodologies.

ICS 361: Artificial Intelligence I (3 Credits)
  - Prerequisites: ICS 241 and ICS 311.
  - Typical Weekday Blocks: TR 12:00 PM - 1:15 PM
  - Faculty Assignment: Peter Washington
  - Student Quality Rating: 4.0 / 5.0
  - Community Notes: Search logic, heuristics, state machines, and basic machine learning structures.
